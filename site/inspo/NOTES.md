# Inspiration for the Vulcan Orbit redesign

Screenshots were taken on 15 Nov 2026 from the live pages. **Take only what the "Take" line says.**

Copy no code, logos, brand colours, photos or text from any of these. The Framer components and CodePen demos are other people's code, and AGENTS.md rule 9 forbids importing outside code. Rebuild every effect from scratch in our own WebGL or Canvas code, and credit the idea (name and URL) in REFERENCES.md.

| File | Source | What it is | Take |
|---|---|---|---|
| 05_cursor_crosshair.jpg, 06_cursor_crosshair_hover.png | Cursor Structule, framer.com/marketplace/components/cursor-structule | A custom cursor: a small dot with thin, full-viewport horizontal and vertical hairlines that follow the pointer.
