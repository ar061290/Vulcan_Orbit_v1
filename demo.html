NASA Space Apps 2026 · Flame in Freefall

In orbit,
nothing
rises.

Without gravity a flame becomes a dim blue ball and smoke drifts wherever the fans push it. Vulcan Orbit finds the fire, simulates how it will spread, and rates the risk, using NASA's own space-station fire data.

Run the demo ↓

Illustration, not data

01

Detect

A camera finds the flame and measures it. A flame sensor beats smoke detection by over a minute.

02

Simulate

That flame goes into our spread model, calibrated on NASA BASS-II and Saffire flight data. Change the air and watch it react.

03

Assess

Size, timing and NASA's alarm times become one risk level, with the reason in plain words.

04

Respond

What NASA's droplet flames tell us about putting a fire out.

Every number has a source.

The full project has the method, the checks, every model's score beside its baseline, and the NASA datasets with their DOIs.
